# CellarPOS: staff quick guide

## Every shift
1. **Sign in**: tap your name, enter your PIN. Never share your PIN.
2. **Till → Open shift**: count the drawer and enter the opening cash. You cannot sell until a shift is open.
3. **Sell**: scan the bottle (or tap the product, then Bottle / Quarter / Shot). Scanning a case barcode adds a full case. Use + / − in the cart.
4. **Customer**: tap *Customer* for account customers or wholesale prices. Walk-ins need nothing.
5. **Age check**: choose "Clearly of legal age" or "ID checked" before completing the sale. If in doubt, ask for ID.
6. **Pay**: enter the amounts. You can split (e.g. Cash 500 + M-Pesa 1,500). *Exact cash* fills the total. Change is calculated for you.
7. **M-Pesa**: enter the customer's number, tap **Send STK**, wait for "Paid" and the code appears by itself. Never type a code from a customer's phone message if you can use STK. Each code works only once.
8. **Receipt**: *Print* for a normal print, *Thermal* for the receipt printer (cash sales also open the drawer).
9. **Close shift**: Till → Close shift, count the drawer, enter the cash. Any difference is recorded as a variance. Print the shift report.

## Things you cannot do alone (ask a manager)
- Give a discount (the manager types their PIN).
- Refund or return items, adjust stock, change prices.

## Money going in or out of the drawer (not sales)
Till → *Cash out* (petty cash, transport, supplier payment, bank deposit) or *Cash in*. Always add a note.

## If the internet or server drops
The header shows **Offline**. Cash sales are saved on the till and sent automatically when the connection returns (header shows "N queued"). Discounts, account sales and M-Pesa STK need the server. Do not clear the browser's data while sales are queued.

## Delivery and phone orders
Orders tab → **+ Order**. Move it along: Start picking → Mark packed → Dispatch (enter the rider) → Mark delivered (check ID at the door). Take payment up front or on delivery.

## Breakages and spillage
Tell a manager straight away. They record it under Stock → Adjust with a reason, so the stock and the audit trail stay correct.
